## Output

The system displays:

- Temperature (Celsius)  
- Humidity (%)  
- Air Quality (ppm approx)  
- Dust Density (ug/m3)  
- Rain Probability (%)  


Data is also printed in Serial Monitor.

## Limitations

- MQ135 values are not calibrated  
- Rain prediction is basic logic  
- No internet connectivity  
- Sensor accuracy depends on environment
